# AGENTS.md

Public-safe rules for AI working on **cyaichi.com** source.

- This repo is public writing only. No unpublished notes. No charter dumps.
- Speak plainly. Lead with the answer. Short sentences. No word salad, vendor poetry, or filler.
- Teach a method, an analysis, or a synthesis. Do not post CVE roundups.
- Do not invent claims, deployments, or accuracy numbers.
- Do not write exploits or malware.
- Observe [OWASP GenAI LLM Top 10 2026](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-genai-llm-top-10-2026/) in any AI or site-generation feature: no secrets in prompts, no unbounded generation loops, validate output before it is published or executed, treat retrieved content as untrusted.
